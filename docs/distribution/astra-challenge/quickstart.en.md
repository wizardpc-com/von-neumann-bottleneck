# Your first session

[简体中文](quickstart.zh-CN.md) · [Game overview](../../../README.en.md)

**Start with the task tree.** Choose English at the top of the home screen. Open the task tree, select the available wiring tutorial, and read its Mission before building. No circuit knowledge is required for the first task; you will learn controls a few at a time.

## Build, check, change

1. Take a component from the open Components panel. Drag it onto the board, or click to place repeated copies. Right-click or Esc cancels placement.
2. Follow the current Mission. Connect compatible ports; crossing wires does not connect them. Thin wires with round ports carry one bit. Wider ribbons with square ports carry multiple bits. The tutorial and handbook introduce notation as it becomes relevant.
3. Debug a particular input to see what happens. Use the full test when you want to check all official cases. A successful single example does not prove the whole circuit.
4. Correct your design. On Mac, use ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z; on Windows, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y. In a text field these shortcuts edit text first.

On Mac, ⌘, opens Settings and ⌃⌘F toggles fullscreen. F10 opens Settings on either platform. Drag tool, Settings and Handbook titles to move their windows; their text and buttons keep normal behavior.

## When something is unclear

Reopen Mission for the task's requirements, or use the illustrated Handbook. Hints are optional and reveal progressively; later stages ask before showing more. They appear separately and do not edit your design.

You can return to the tree and try another available branch. Locked tasks show what comes first. Continue locates your recent task; opening the tree does not reset your work. Programs preserve unfinished drafts separately from the source you have applied.

## Stop whenever you want

Use Settings → Quit, then reopen and Continue. Keep a backup before installing an update; do not delete your saves to try a new build. Use named designs when you want to keep alternative circuits.

## Tell us about a moment

Use F8 or Feedback inside a task, even if you have not finished it. A rating is optional. Useful feedback is concrete: “I dragged this component here and expected it to stay.” Notes stay local until you explicitly share them. No server or account is needed to play.

For a startup problem, report the build ID, operating system and steps in a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/wizardpc-com/von-neumann-bottleneck/issues/new/choose). Do not attach your entire save folder or private system logs by default. Current platform limitations are in the package's KNOWN-ISSUES file; the source README is not a promise that every exported platform has passed acceptance.
