## Limits and decisions

- Four original symbols; silent visual medium, color plus shape. Final art/audio
  and educational effectiveness require human review.
- Frozen count model, order 0/1/2; deterministic tie-break and integer sampler.
- Sequential teaching costs, cold runs; no invented overlap or background queue.
- Prediction future material remains outside model inputs and public UI state;
  revealed checks are marked seen. Generation has no target truth.
- Snapshot playback survives incompatible recipe versions; incompatible data
  blocks rewriting rather than silently substituting current output.
