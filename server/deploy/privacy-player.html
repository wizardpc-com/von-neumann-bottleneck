冯·诺依曼瓶颈 · 玩家反馈隐私说明

反馈服务运营者：yanruiqing。数据处理或删除问题请联系 yanruiqing@agent.qq.com。

发送一条意见

游戏和本机保存可离线使用。点「保存到本机」只保存意见。点「发送反馈」会将这条意见加入发送队列，未收到回执的意见可能在你下次打开游戏后重试。

发送内容可以包含最多 240 字的意见、所选问题类型和评分。游戏还会附上相关任务、会话、访问或运行标识、游戏或测试模式、来源类别、构建和试用批次、适用的任务或模型版本、分享同意版本和时间，以及你选择填写的经验分组。服务使用随机生成的安装标识和删除凭据来识别该安装、生成回执、去重和处理删除。请避免在意见中填写个人信息。

可选统计与成绩

  • 统计上传默认关闭。可选的基础统计涵盖同意后开始的任务访问，包括完成情况、时间、汇总计数和反馈时刻类别。
  • 详细统计是另一个设置选项，可分享限定范围的编辑、运行、用例结果、时间、提示和地图操作。统计记录不含意见正文。
  • 在提供实验成绩分享的版本中，成绩需单独允许并主动提交。发送的是限定的成绩指标和版本等上下文。

以上在线渠道不附带程序全文、电路或作品文件、截图、剪贴板内容或本机文件路径。如果你另行导出并分享本机 ZIP，文件可能包含游戏现场和存档。

保留、查看与删除

接收库按 30 天期限清理收到的意见、统计和成绩。运营者在私有报告中查看记录和处理意见。报告及批注随维护清理和更新，可能晚于接收库中的删除。报告没有公开访问入口。

你可以在游戏的反馈设置中停止分享并请求删除已上传数据。服务确认后，该安装标识在接收库中的上传记录会被删除。游戏中的本机意见和进度仍保留。已有备份不会立刻擦除，服务器本机备份按 30 天期限轮换，清理可能等到下一次成功维护。为防止从旧备份恢复已删数据，安装标识、删除凭据的哈希和删除时间可能保留更久。

Von Neumann Bottleneck · Player Feedback Privacy Notice

Feedback service operator: yanruiqing. For questions about data handling or deletion, email yanruiqing@agent.qq.com.

Sending an opinion

You can play and save locally while offline. Save on this device keeps the opinion on your device. Send feedback queues that opinion. If a receipt is still pending, the game may retry after you reopen it.

An opinion can include up to 240 characters of text, a selected category and ratings. The game also attaches relevant task, session, visit or run IDs, Game or Test mode, source category, build and playtest batch, applicable task or model versions, consent version and time, and any experience group you select. The service uses a random installation ID and deletion credential to identify the installation, issue receipts, deduplicate and process deletion. Please leave personal information out of your opinion.

Optional statistics and scores

  • Statistics uploads start off. Optional Basic statistics cover task visits started after consent, including completion, time, summary counts and feedback moment categories.
  • Detailed statistics are a separate setting and can share limited editing, run, case-result, timing, hint and map actions. Statistics exclude opinion text.
  • Where experimental score sharing is available, it requires separate permission and an explicit submission. It sends bounded score metrics and context such as versions.

These online channels exclude full programs, circuit or work files, screenshots, clipboard contents and local file paths. A local ZIP that you export and share separately may contain game captures and saves.

Retention, access and deletion

The receiver removes opinions, statistics and scores on a 30-day retention schedule. The operator reviews submissions in private reports. Reports and review annotations are purged and refreshed during maintenance, which can happen after removal from the receiving database.

In feedback settings you can stop sharing and request deletion of uploaded data. Once the service confirms deletion, it removes uploads for that installation ID from the receiving database. Your local opinions and progress remain on your device. Existing backups are rotated on a 30-day schedule and may await the next successful maintenance run. The installation ID, deletion-credential hash and deletion time may be retained longer to prevent deleted records from returning during a restore.
